Professional Introduction

As I prepare to transition from nursing curriculum to a registered nurse, my professional identity comes from a place of resilience, always statying curious in the field of nursing, and extreme work ethic. Nursing is such a field that it requires adapting to the environment at all times. I view every clinical challenge as an oppurtunity to expand my judgement and improve my skills. I am very committed to being an lifelong learner and consistently seeking out feedback so that I am able to deliver the highest standard of patient-centered care from day one.

My passion lies in the high-acuity critical care, specifically within the intensive care setting. I am drawn to the complex care of critically ill patients, where rapid assessments, content mastery, and swift intervention all impact survical and recovery. Navigating this fast-paced, high-stakes environment intrigues me and I thrive on the problem solving that is needed to support patients and their families through their most vulnerable moments.

My nursing practice will be anchored by honesty, grit, and a deep commitment to teamwork. In critical care, collaboration is key and I beleive that clear communication, mutual accountability, and humility creates the safest environment for patients. Honesty guides my advocacy and integrity at the bedside, while grit is there so that I remain composed, persistent, and fully present during my shifts as a nurse.

Following graduation, my primary short-term goal is to successfully completely my nurse residency in my CVICU unit position. Over my first year, I aim to master hemodynamic monitoring and really bridge together medication titration, life support technologies while building strong clinical autonomy. Beyond the technical proficiency, I want to be able to establish myself as a dependable, proactive teammate who brings focus and a positive presence to the unit while ultimately preparing for my CCRN certification once I feel competent in my skills.
